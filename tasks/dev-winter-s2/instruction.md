You run a commercial greenhouse facility in the Netherlands for 14 winter days.

One energy centre (a gas CHP, a gas boiler, a heat buffer, a CO2 store and a grid
connection) serves three compartments. Compartments A and B grow broccoli microgreens
and compartment C grows radish microgreens, each in several batches of different ages.
You also run the business: you order CO2, seed and spare parts, which arrive days
later; you hire and release staff; sowing and harvesting use your crew's worker-hours;
damage is repaired by contractors you book.

Your goal is the highest net profit in EUR at the end of the last hour: harvest revenue
plus electricity sold, minus gas, electricity bought, CO2, wages, hiring, contractors,
parts and orders. Crop still standing and parts left in stock at the end are worth nothing.

How it works:
- You take one turn a day, at 06:00. Below is everything you are shown before your
  first call: every rule (mechanisms, formulas, prices, lead times, crop rules, event
  rates, news signals, the published ranges of hidden facility parameters) and this
  morning's briefing. There are no tools to read anything.
- Each day you make exactly one call, `submit_day`, with up to 5 actions
  (`rules.turn.actions` lists them); a list of harvests, plantings or orders is one
  action. A refused action changes nothing and the others still apply.
- The call then runs the day (06:00-18:00, the crew at work) on your day settings and the
  night (18:00-06:00) on your night schedule (`set_night_schedule`), with nobody acting.
  Its result is the next morning's briefing: what happened overnight, the status now,
  news and the forecast.
- Settings stand until you change them. The facility starts on an anti-freeze baseline
  (5 C, no CO2, lamps off) by day and by night.
- Damage (a failed boiler, glass broken by a storm) stays until a contractor repairs it
  with the parts on site. Forecasts and news give warnings; some are false alarms.
- You have 140 tool calls in total. If you stop calling tools or they are used
  up, the remaining days run with your last settings and nobody acting: nothing more is
  ordered, planted, harvested or repaired.
- The episode lasts 336 hours and starts on day 1 at 06:00. Plan the whole
  horizon: several decisions only pay off days later, and some cannot be made late.

Every action goes through the tools of the MCP server `env` (`http://sim:8000/mcp`), which is already connected. Nothing in this container's filesystem affects your result.

- Each tool call spends one operation, unless the tool's description says otherwise. A call rejected for malformed arguments spends nothing.
- Every result is JSON with `ok`, then `result` or `error`, then a `header` with the operations left (`ops_left`) and status. When `header.done` is true the episode is over.
- `header.calls_left` is the number of tool calls you have left in the whole episode, every request counted. `header.ops_left` only counts operations in the current window.
- There is no reset and no second attempt. Your result is computed from the state of the world when the episode ends.
