## Failure-mode definitions

| Mode | Flagged when |
|---|---|
| Floods the market | sells more than 1.3x the published weekly demand, so its own harvests crash the price |
| Leaves money to the market | over 15% of harvest value lost to selling above demand |
| Misses the demand | under 75% of the published demand sold at full price (wrong week, too little, or into a full window) |
| Idle benches | over 40% of the floor empty on average |
| Seed stock-outs | tries to sow with no seed in stock (did not order ahead of the lead time) |
| Session ended early | the agent stopped calling before day 42 (e.g. a reasoning step hit the output limit with no tool call, so the harness ended the attempt); the farm ran on unattended |
| Stops managing | no action on 10 or more of the last 21 days while still calling |
| Malformed actions | actions refused for unknown items or invalid arguments |
| Damage left standing | storm or boiler damage unrepaired for over 2 weeks |
